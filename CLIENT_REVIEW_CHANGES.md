## 10. Content rewrite: simpler English that sells better

After building the structure, we reviewed the words on the main pages. A lot of the earlier copy used long, fancy phrases. That can look polished to a designer, but it is hard for a busy customer, carpenter or contractor to understand quickly.

### Why we changed the content

- **Customers decide fast.** Clear words help them trust you and take the next step (call, enquire, visit).
- **Your buyers are practical.** They care about grade, size, brand and delivery, not poetic lines.
- **Simple English ranks and converts.** Search engines and people both prefer clear, useful text.
- **One voice across the site.** Home, About, Products, Plywood, Hardware, Laminates, Veneers, Brands and Contact now feel consistent.

### What we changed (examples)

| Before (hard to read) | After (simple and clear) |
|---|---|
| “A Legacy Built on Trust. A Journey Driven by Relationships.” | “Trusted Since 1998. Built on Relationships.” |
| “Precision in Every Detail.” / “Natural Character. Matched Runs.” | “Fittings That Work Every Day.” / “Real Wood Look. Easy to Match.” |
| “Where our materials go to work.” | “Where our materials are used.” |
| “Eight reasons a carpenter sends the next client.” | “Eight reasons customers come back.” |
| Heavy em-dash style lines that felt AI-written | Removed. Sentences now use normal commas and full stops. |

### Where content was updated

- **Home:** hero support text, about teaser, product lines, plywood/hardware/laminates/veneers blocks, brands, why us, uses, reviews, quality, visit/quote  
- **About:** hero, story chapters, founders, values, closing CTA  
- **Products hub + Plywood + Hardware + Laminates + Veneers:** heroes, section intros, help CTAs  
- **Brands + Contact:** heroes, partner copy, form intro, sales CTA  

**Impact:** Visitors understand faster what you sell, where you are, and what to do next. That reduces confusion and supports more enquiries.

## 11. SEO: titles, meta descriptions, keywords and structured data

SEO means helping Google (and other search engines) understand your pages and show them when people search for plywood, hardware, laminates or veneers in Pune. We built a proper SEO helper in the site that sets title, description, keywords, Open Graph tags, canonical URL and JSON-LD for each important page.

### Why this matters for Yashply

- Local buyers search things like “plywood Pune”, “BWP plywood”, “hinges Pune”, “laminates Pune”.
- A strong title and description improve click-through from search results.
- LocalBusiness / ContactPage data helps Google connect phone, address and hours to your brand.
- Product list pages tell Google what you sell, with clean URLs for each line.

### What we set on each key page

| Tag / field | What it is | Why we use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Page title** | The blue link text in Google | Includes product + city + brand so the right searches match |
| **Meta description** | Short summary under the title | Explains the offer in one breath and invites the click |
| **Keywords** | Supporting keyword list | Covers local and product terms (plywood Pune, BWP, Hettich, Merino, etc.) |
| **Canonical URL** | Preferred page address | Avoids duplicate-page confusion |
| **Open Graph / Twitter** | Share preview title, text, image | Looks clean when shared on WhatsApp / social |
| **JSON-LD (schema)** | Machine-readable business / page data | Helps Google understand LocalBusiness, ContactPage, product lists and brands |
| **Image alt text** | Text for images | Accessibility + extra context for search |
